Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
What are Numarqo PWAs?
Numarqo builds high-performance Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) combining casual gaming and high-utility productivity tools optimized for lightweight mobile usage across global markets.
Are Numarqo apps free to use?
Yes, our entire suite of casual games and utility tools is available as lightweight web applications, with select native packages distributed via the Google Play Store.
How do I secure my data in Secure Calculator Vault?
All sensitive files and notes stored inside the Numarqo Secure Vault are encrypted locally on your device storage using robust client-side algorithms. We never upload your private files to external servers.
